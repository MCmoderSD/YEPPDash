How a draw works

  1. Open puts the reward live in your channel. Viewers redeem it to enter.
  2. Close stops new entries. The wheel only spins while registration is closed, so nobody can enter mid-draw and shift everybody's odds.
  3. Draw a winner spins the wheel and records the result. A bigger slice is a bigger multiplier.
  4. Reset goes back to draft and clears every entry and winner. There is no undo.

The same person can be drawn twice — take them off the wheel to stop that. The browser source above always shows this giveaway; add it to OBS once and leave it.
