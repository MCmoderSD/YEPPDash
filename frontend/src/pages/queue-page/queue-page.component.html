Chat commands

Chat and this page work on the same queue, so anything either side does shows up on the other within a second or two.

!queue join
Takes a place at the back, if the queue is open and the requirement is met
!queue leave
Gives that place up
!queue open
Lets chat join
!queue close
Turns anyone new away — everybody already waiting keeps their place
!queue reset
Empties it, without asking twice — !queue clear does the same
!queue next
Says who is up — it does not move the queue on
!queue list
Reads out the order — !queue status, and !queue on its own, do the same
!queue position
Answers with the caller's place

shield open, close and reset are yours and your moderators'. Taking the person at the front off, and changing the order, are only possible from this page.
