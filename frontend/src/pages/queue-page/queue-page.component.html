Chat commands

Chat and this page work on the same queue, so anything either side does shows up on the other within a second or two.

!queue join
Takes a place at the back, if the queue is open
!queue leave
Gives that place up
!queue status
Answers with the caller's place
!queue list
Reads out the order
!queue next
Says who is up — it does not move the queue on

shield !queue open, close, reset and clear are yours and your moderators'. Taking the person at the front off, and changing the order, are only possible from this page.
