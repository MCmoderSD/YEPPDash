The service

YEPPDash lets Twitch broadcasters manage YEPPBot for their channel from a browser. It is a free, solo-developed, open-source hobby project, not a commercial product. Today it lets you:

  • have YEPPBot join or leave your channel;
  • view and manage your channel's moderators and VIPs, and view its editors;
  • view your followers, the users currently in chat, and the accounts you have blocked or banned, and lift a block or ban;
  • add, edit, reorder, delete, import and export your channel's quotes;
  • create, edit, enable, disable and delete your channel's custom commands;
  • set your own birthday and see the birthdays of your followers who set one;
  • view the BDSM test results of followers who took YEPPBot's test in chat;
  • run channel point giveaways in your channel, and see who entered and who was drawn.

Which of these are available can change as the project develops.
