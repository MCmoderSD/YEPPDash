Privacy Policy

This policy explains what data YEPPDash processes when you use it, why, and what rights you have under the GDPR. YEPPDash is a solo-developed, open-source project — there is no marketing department, no analytics vendor, and no data broker involved.

Controller

Seraphin Berger ("MCmoderSD"), contact: business@mcmodersd.de. See the Imprint for full details.

What data is processed

  • Twitch account data. When you log in, YEPPDash receives your Twitch user ID, login name, display name, profile image and (if granted) email address from Twitch via OAuth. This is used only to identify you and to display your account in the dashboard.
  • Twitch access & refresh tokens. Stored encrypted at rest, keyed to your Twitch user ID, and used solely to act on your behalf against the Twitch API for the features you use (e.g. managing your channel's bot, moderators, or VIPs). Tokens are never shared with anyone other than Twitch's own API.
  • Session cookie. A single yeppdash.session cookie (HttpOnly, Secure, SameSite=Lax, valid up to 14 days) keeps you signed in. It contains no tracking identifiers and is not used for advertising or analytics.
  • Server logs. Standard technical logs (IP address, timestamp, requested URL, status code) are kept briefly for security and debugging purposes, then rotated out.

No other tracking, analytics, or advertising cookies are set.

Legal basis

Processing is based on Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR (necessary to provide the dashboard you requested) and, for server logs, Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR (legitimate interest in operating a secure, reliable service).

Who else sees this data

Twitch Interactive, Inc. receives the requests YEPPDash makes on your behalf as part of the OAuth flow and API calls — see Twitch's own Privacy Notice for how they handle that. Your data is otherwise not sold, rented, or shared with any third party.

Data retention

Account and token data is kept until you disconnect YEPPDash, revoke access via Twitch, or request deletion. Server logs are retained only briefly before being rotated out.

Your rights

Under the GDPR, you have the right to:

  • access the data held about you;
  • request correction or deletion of your data;
  • restrict or object to processing;
  • receive your data in a portable format;
  • lodge a complaint with a supervisory authority.

To exercise any of these, or to have your account data deleted, contact business@mcmodersd.de. You can also revoke YEPPDash's access at any time from your Twitch connected-apps settings.

Security

All traffic is served over HTTPS. Stored Twitch tokens are encrypted at rest, and the session cookie is HttpOnly and Secure so it cannot be read or sent over an insecure connection.

Children's privacy

YEPPDash is not directed at children and is not knowingly used to collect data from children.
