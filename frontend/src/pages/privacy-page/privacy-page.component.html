Privacy Policy

This policy explains what data YEPPDash processes when you use it, why, and what rights you have under the GDPR. YEPPDash is a solo-developed, open-source project — there is no marketing department, no analytics vendor, and no data broker involved.

YEPPDash and YEPPBot

YEPPDash is the web dashboard for YEPPBot. Both are operated by the same person and share one database: YEPPDash mostly reads and edits data that YEPPBot already collected in your channel, and tells YEPPBot when to join or leave a channel or to reload its custom commands. Where this policy says data is "stored", it is stored in that shared database.

What data is processed

Your own account

  • Twitch account data. When you log in, YEPPDash receives your Twitch user ID, login name, display name, account type, description, profile and offline images, account creation date and (because the user:read:email scope is granted) your email address. This identifies you and fills in your account view in the dashboard. It is read fresh from Twitch on each session check rather than kept in a profile of our own.
  • Twitch access & refresh tokens. Stored encrypted at rest (AES-256-GCM), keyed to your Twitch user ID, and used solely to act on your behalf against the Twitch API for the features you use. Tokens are never shared with anyone other than Twitch's own API.
  • Session cookie. A single yeppdash.session cookie (HttpOnly, Secure, SameSite=Lax, valid up to 14 days) keeps you signed in. It contains no tracking identifiers and is not used for advertising or analytics.
  • Server logs. Standard technical logs (IP address, timestamp, requested URL, status code) are kept briefly for security and debugging purposes, then rotated out.

Channel data read live from Twitch

For the channel you own, YEPPDash asks Twitch for the lists it needs to render the dashboard: moderators, VIPs, channel editors, followers, users you have blocked, ban status, and the users currently in chat. For the accounts on those lists it also reads the public Twitch profile and chat colour so they can be shown with a name and avatar instead of a bare ID.

These lists are not written to the database. They are held in the server's memory for a short while so the dashboard does not have to re-fetch them on every click — including a fetch started right after you log in — and they are dropped when the server restarts or when you change a role through the dashboard.

Channel data stored in the shared database

  • Birthdays. The day, month and year you set for yourself, stored against your Twitch user ID. Broadcasters see a birthday list covering themselves and their followers who have set one, so YEPPBot can congratulate them.
  • Quotes. The quote text and its timestamp, per channel. You can add, edit, reorder and delete them, and export them to, or import them from, a spreadsheet file.
  • Custom commands. Command name, aliases, response message, active flag, response type and required user level, per channel.
  • BDSM test results. Where a user has taken YEPPBot's BDSM test in chat, the result is stored with their Twitch user ID, a timestamp, the stated gender and age group, and a score per trait. YEPPDash only displays these results — it never creates or edits them. Broadcasters can view the results of their own followers; nobody else can read them through YEPPDash.

No tracking, analytics, or advertising cookies are set, and no data is stored in your browser.

Special category data

BDSM test results say something about a person's sex life and therefore count as a special category of personal data under Art. 9 GDPR. They are processed only on the basis of explicit consent under Art. 9(2)(a) GDPR, given by choosing to take the test in chat — the test is never run for you, and if you never take it there is nothing to show. That consent can be withdrawn at any time by asking for the result to be deleted, using the contact address below.

If you are a broadcaster: results of your followers are shown to you because they consented to the test in a channel context. You are responsible for handling what you see there lawfully and discreetly, and for not republishing it.

Legal basis

  • Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR — account, token, session and channel data needed to provide the dashboard you asked for;
  • Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR — server logs, for the legitimate interest in operating a secure, reliable service;
  • Art. 6(1)(a) and Art. 9(2)(a) GDPR — BDSM test results, on explicit consent given in chat.

Who else sees this data

Twitch Interactive, Inc. receives the requests YEPPDash makes on your behalf as part of the OAuth flow and API calls — see Twitch's own Privacy Notice for how they handle that. The YEPPBot instance, run by the same operator, receives your channel's user ID when you have it join or leave, or when its custom commands are reloaded. Broadcasters see the channel data described above for their own channel. Your data is otherwise not sold, rented, or shared with any third party.

Data retention

Tokens are kept until you sign out, revoke access via Twitch, or request deletion; signing out revokes the token and removes the stored row. Channel data — birthdays, quotes, custom commands and BDSM results — belongs to YEPPBot and stays in the shared database until it is deleted in the dashboard, in chat, or on request; revoking YEPPDash's Twitch access alone does not remove it. The live channel lists live only in memory, and server logs are rotated out after a short period.

Your rights

Under the GDPR, you have the right to:

  • access the data held about you;
  • request correction or deletion of your data;
  • withdraw consent you have given, without affecting processing already carried out;
  • restrict or object to processing;
  • receive your data in a portable format;
  • lodge a complaint with a supervisory authority.

To exercise any of these, or to have your account data deleted, contact business@mcmodersd.de. You can also revoke YEPPDash's access at any time from your Twitch connected-apps settings.

Security

All traffic is served over HTTPS. Stored Twitch tokens are encrypted at rest, and the session cookie is HttpOnly and Secure so it cannot be read or sent over an insecure connection. Every dashboard endpoint checks that the signed-in account matches the channel being asked about, so one account cannot read another's channel data.

Children's privacy

YEPPDash is not directed at children and is not knowingly used to collect data from children.
